# MO-200 outline notes

Source: [MO-200 skills outline](https://arch-center.azureedge.net/Learning/Credentials/mo-200-microsoft-excel-2019-skills-measured.pdf) — accessed 2026-11-24.

- The outline groups the assessed Excel work into five areas: worksheets/workbooks, data cells/ranges, tables/table data, formulas/functions, and charts.
- Its workbook material includes navigation, views, saving in alternate formats, and worksheet-level setup. The project limits this MVP criterion to spoken explanations of sheets, tabs, saving, and moving around a workbook.
- Its cells-and-ranges material supports evaluating reference concepts and the relationship between formulas and locations in a grid.
- Its table, formula, and chart areas are the anchors for the corresponding MVP criteria. The six project criteria split cell/range understanding from formula/function understanding to make voice scoring clearer.
